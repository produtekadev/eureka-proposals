# Playbook: what has worked

From accepted proposals (Nickel AI, Ofiuko, Rojo Estudio Creativo, SISU, Trolley.ai). Use it to shape scope and price, not as a script.

## Structure that closes

1. **Context in their words.** Who they are (size, team, tools), 3 pains with evidence, a quote, why now ("the opportunity"). Clients accepted when they recognized themselves in this section.
2. **A clear focus.** Federico's proposal worked because it picked one North Star (the founder's cockpit) instead of promising all 12 departments at once.
3. **Phases with separate prices.** Every accepted proposal split the work into phases or modules, each with its own price. Discovery or audit first, then build, then optional layers.
4. **Essential vs. optional.** Mark what the project needs (`essential`), what we advise (`recommended`) and nice-to-haves (`optional`). Clients can switch optional pieces off on the page, which keeps the conversation going when the budget is tight.
5. **Included / not included.** Always both. The "not included" list prevents scope creep later (licenses, external automations, extra modules, ongoing support).
6. **Guarantee.** 1 month (Rojo) to 3 months (SISU) of fixes within the delivered scope.
7. **Team and clients.** The people assigned, then the client logo grid.
8. **One clear call to action.** Accept on the page, then deposit by bank transfer, then book the kickoff.

## Pricing references (USD)

| Piece | Typical price | Duration | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workspace audit and report | 1,500 | 2 to 3 weeks | Nickel AI |
| Discovery and roadmap (large org) | 1,500 | 2 weeks | Trolley.ai Phase 1 |
| Core implementation (small team, one operating system) | 1,500 | 3 to 5 weeks | Ofiuko, SISU, Rojo |
| Implementation bundle after an audit | 1,000 | after report | Nickel AI |
| Notion AI setup (agents, prompt library, training) | 300 | in parallel | SISU (optional) |
| Data migration from Drive or Sheets | 500 | about 1 week | Ofiuko (optional) |
| Monthly build for large orgs (2 departments a month) | 4,000 to 6,000 a month, 5,000 average | monthly | Trolley.ai Phase 2 |

- Small teams (under 15 people): total usually USD 1,200 to 2,500.
- Credit what the client already paid for earlier work (Rojo: USD 300 discounted).
- As Notion Consulting Partners we can offer months of Notion AI Business at no cost (Rojo: 6 months, SISU: 3 months). Mention it as a bonus when it applies; confirm with the user first.
- If asked for hours, give an average with a range (Trolley.ai: 125 hours a month on average, 100 to 150).

## Payment and terms

- Default: 50% when they accept, 50% on delivery. Monthly builds: paid at the start of each month. Discovery for big projects: 100% upfront.
- Bank transfer. No card payments by default (no commissions).
- Valid for about two weeks. Projected start: the week after acceptance.
- NDA: offer it when the client handles sensitive data (health, finance).

## Wording that worked

- Section titles: short, two lines, second line in italics (`What we<br><em>heard.</em>`, `Two phases,<br><em>clear prices.</em>`).
- Pains: a bold short name, one line in their words, one line of consequence.
- "Opportunity": why now (an upcoming hire, a growth moment, a failed tool), in 2 sentences.
- Closing: "We're ready to build *your Eureka moment.*" / "Listos para construir *tu momento Eureka.*"
