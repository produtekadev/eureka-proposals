Section 01

What we
heard.

A group of three companies where requests, approvals and decisions still travel through Slack, WhatsApp and email. The goal is to run it all from one place.

3companies: one main company and two sister companies
160people in the main company, plus 12 and 2 in the sister companies
12departments with very different adoption levels
The challenge

Three things slowing down the operation.

01
Visibility

The owner has nowhere to see the operation

What she needs to decide doesn't live in one place.

Requests, approvals and tasks reach her through Slack, WhatsApp and email. There is no single place that shows the three companies separately and together, and updates as each team does its work.

02
Adoption

Twelve teams, no integrated operating system

Not every department starts from the same place.

Some teams have half-built systems of their own, others have no tools at all, and some resist the change. Some people work across all three companies; others should only see one.

03
Speed

The project needs a different pace

The project has started, but it is moving slower than expected.

The owner's view exists, several teams have been pre-screened and two are in construction. The time difference and part-time dedication slowed things down. We don't start from zero: we audit what exists and reuse what works.

“The only thing a person should have to open when they clock in is Notion, with everything they need to do right there.”

Federico González, on the goal of the project
Section 02 · North Star

The founder's
cockpit.

Bringing all 12 teams into Notion with every requirement in six months isn't realistic. We propose a clear North Star: the cockpit the owner uses to run all three companies. Each department is built to feed what that cockpit needs to see.

Illustrative view. Departments and metrics are defined in Phase 1.

What the cockpit holds

Three things in one view.

Approvals

Requests from all three companies with attachment, reason, who and when. Approve, decline or ask for more information.

Tasks

What other executives assign her, with a due date: review a contract, approve a payment.

Metrics by department

The metrics she needs to decide, filtered by company or combined.

“It's literally a cockpit with every gauge measuring absolutely everything.”

Federico González

What finished means

A finished department includes:

  • ✓Its Notion workspace, with databases and views for day-to-day work
  • ✓Its metrics connected to the cockpit
  • ✓The integrations defined in the roadmap
  • ✓Training with the team
  • ✓Documentation and guides

Integrations that serve the cockpit. Many tools can be connected, but we prioritize the integrations that feed the North Star, not every team's wish list. Each one is scoped in the roadmap, subject to technical feasibility (an available API or connector). Large integrations and migrations from other tools are also defined there, since they shape each department's price.

What we need from your side: one owner per department with time for interviews and training, access to the tools being integrated, and a point of contact to approve the roadmap.
