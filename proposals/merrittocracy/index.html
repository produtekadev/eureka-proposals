Section 01

What we
heard.

The Merrittocracy is a social justice agency that brings strategy, research, creative work and implementation together. The team runs its core systems in Notion, with important files linked to Google Drive and Claude helping with meeting minutes and incoming emails. As the team grows, you want the workspace to stay secure, efficient and easy to navigate.

7people on the team, and growing
5core systems in Notion: programmes, CRM, finances, capacity and information
1teamspace holding all of it
The challenge

Three things to sort out before the team grows.

01
Access

One teamspace for everything

"I haven't properly used team spaces."

Access is decided page by page. That gets harder to keep secure with every new person, especially around sensitive data.

02
Navigation

Long dashboards that load slowly

"It's a big long list."

Graphs, projects and several views on one page take time to load and are hard for a new team member to read.

03
AI and tools

Updates that are hard to find

"Is there anything on the Business plan that we should be using that we're not?"

Claude's project updates land as comments, which get harder to find as they pile up. Meeting notes live in Gemini, outside the workspace.

“We've got everything we need. We just need to make it much more efficiently structured.”

Marcus Wright, Head of Projects
The opportunity

You already have the core systems and the information in place. Before adding more processes, an audit shows how to make what you have clearer, more secure and easier for a growing team to use, without over-engineering the workspace.
