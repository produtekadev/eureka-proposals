Section 01

What we
heard.

Biomed is a three-person recruitment agency moving to Grok as its central AI. Recruiterflow stays as the ATS, but everything else is spread across Planner, SharePoint, OneDrive, OneNote and a new Notion workspace, and the agents can only be as good as the place they read from.

3people: you, Chris and Elizabeth
10k+candidates in Recruiterflow
5tools holding tasks, files and notes today
The challenge

Three things holding back the setup.

01
Files and notes

All over the place

"OneNote is out of control."

Ten years of notes, plus SharePoint and OneDrive, with no clear rule for what lives where. Grok can't find what nobody can find.

02
Tasks

Every new job starts in Planner

"It's a lot of manual stuff."

Assigning tasks to Chris and Elizabeth for each new role is done by hand, and Planner doesn't talk to Grok.

03
Team

Only you are in

"They're not even using Grok yet."

Chris and Elizabeth aren't on Notion yet, so the system only works for one person.

“I do like how Grok can just pull things without me going into Notion, but I also wanna keep Notion organized and clean, too.”

Ian Cooper, Founder
The opportunity

You're about to build agents for recruiting, metrics and sales. If Notion is organized first, every agent starts with the right context instead of each one needing its own fix later.

Section 02

Where we'd
start.

One organized Notion that both your team and your AI agents work from: every task, document and decision in one known place. You mentioned a lot of good ideas. We'd start with the foundation they all depend on.

What you asked for
  • Organize Notion, with personal and private space
  • Notion as the brain for Grok
  • Task assignment for every new job
  • Centralize meeting recordings
  • Daily metrics snapshot from Recruiterflow
  • Clean up OneNote, SharePoint and OneDrive
  • Agents for recruiting, sales and finance
What we recommend
  • Clean architecture, with personal and team spaces
  • Context layer any connected agent can use
  • Automated tasks for each job type, plus team training
  • Meetings in one place, keeping the agents' memory current
  • Metrics and agents next, once the foundation is in place

Recruiterflow's metrics aren't reliable yet, so a dashboard built now would show the wrong numbers. Agents built on an organized Notion will need less rework later.
