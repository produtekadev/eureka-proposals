# Playbook

What has worked in accepted proposals (Nickel AI, Ofiuko, Rojo, SISU, Trolley.ai, SYNC). Use it to shape the proposal, not as a script. Add a rule here only when the team asks for it or it shows up in two proposals.

## Structure

Order: Cover, Context, Goal, Proposal, Team, Start. Minimal, important things first.

1. **Cover.** Minimal (`cover.goal`): the goal and "Duration & price" first, who and dates in one small line. Client logo in black if we have it.
2. **Context in their words.** Who they are, 3 pains (bold name, their words, the consequence), a quote, why now. Clients accept when they recognize themselves here.
3. **Goal, not features.** One statement and benefit cards (`focus.benefits`): what the business looks like after working with us. One North Star beats promising every department at once.
4. **Proposal.** Phases with their own price, all in one fixed total. Audit or discovery first, then build, then training. Each phase: illustration, deliverable ticks, "Your part".
5. **Included / not included.** Always both; clients read "not included" closely.
6. **Guarantee.** 2 weeks to 3 months of fixes within the delivered scope.
7. **Team and client logos.**
8. **Start.** Next steps (accept, deposit, NDA if needed, kickoff) and accept on the page.

## Design defaults

- Illustrations (`template/assets/illustrations/`): `map` audit, `build` build, `scale` training, `team-work` next to the closing, `too-many-apps` in Context between the pains title and the cards. Phase drawings are small, on the left of the ticks.
- Totals box shows only Total and Duration; the 50/50 split goes in the text.
- **Optional, only when the team asks:** a Notion AI chat window in Goal (`focus.mockups`, type `chat`) with their real question, the answer and its sources, example questions, and what Notion reads. One box, never several.

## Prices (USD)

- Standard: **audit or discovery 500**, **training 300**. The build phase carries the rest.
- Small teams (under 15 people): total usually 1,200 to 3,500. Migrations sit at the high end.

| Piece | Typical price | Duration | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workspace audit and report | 1,500 | 2 to 3 weeks | Nickel AI |
| Discovery and roadmap (large org) | 1,500 | 2 weeks | Trolley.ai |
| Core implementation (small team) | 1,500 to 2,400 | 3 to 5 weeks | Ofiuko, SISU, Rojo, SYNC |
| Data migration from Drive or Sheets | 500 | about 1 week | Ofiuko |
| Monthly build for large orgs | 4,000 to 6,000 a month | monthly | Trolley.ai |

- Credit what the client already paid for earlier work.
- Free months of Notion Business (partner benefit): confirm with the team and check the client didn't already get them. Show them near the price, on a new workspace.
- If asked for hours, give an average with a range.

## Payment and terms

- 50% on accepting, 50% on delivery. Monthly builds: start of each month. Big discoveries: 100% upfront.
- Bank transfer, or card through Stripe with a 4% fee.
- Valid about two weeks; start the week after acceptance. NDA before the kickoff when they share sensitive data.

## Wording

Use:
- Two-line titles, second line in italics. Proposal section: "What we'll build / *and what it costs.*" (ES: "Lo que vamos a construir / *y lo que cuesta.*").
- Closing: "We're ready to build *your Eureka moment.*" / "Listos para construir *tu momento Eureka.*"

Avoid:
- Titles that need their detail to make sense ("Optimized operating guide" → "Your AI tools connected").
- "Products database" for product knowledge → "Product catalog and specs".
- "No AI credits" (Notion agents use credits) → "One plan, everything included".
- Vague exclusions → "Additional custom AI agents", "Moving media files out of Google Drive".
- Naming one of their team members in a pain.
