Section 01

What we
heard.

SYNC Performance is an apparel company running ops, sales and product across Monday, Google Drive, Slack, Missive, Shopify and Granola.

~10people on the team by January
30Monday boards actively maintained, by your estimate
9+tools in use: Monday, Drive, Slack, Missive, Shopify and Granola, plus Claude, ChatGPT and Grok
The challenge

Three things slowing down the team.

01
Context

No central hub

"Every little instance of communication doesn't have a central database."

Updates live in Slack threads and personal AI chats. When the person who owns an update is out, it doesn't go out, and nobody can ask their AI what the rest of the team did today.

02
Product

Knowledge stuck in Drive

"Why is this jacket have this fabric this year and it had this other fabric last year?"

Tech packs sit in Drive folders, so answers depend on finding the right file or asking your VP of product directly.

03
Tools

Monday is slow and costly

"We've been frustrated with the speed of Monday."

Pages take forever to load and updates get lost. You pay for the CRM and work management separately, and AI credits on top of that.

“We see the benefit potential of Notion, but we just haven't been able to allocate enough time to it.”

Alex Leever
The opportunity

You and Katie already proved Notion works for your team. Peak season is the right time for us to build the rest, so everyone starts January with their department set up and the training to use it.
